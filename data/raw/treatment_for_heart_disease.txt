Coronary Heart Disease Treatment
Coronary Heart Disease Treatment Overview

Treatment for coronary heart disease depends on the severity of symptoms and any other existing health conditions. Symptoms may worsen over time as plaque builds up in the coronary arteries. Treatment typically includes a combination of heart-healthy lifestyle changes, medications, and medical procedures to prevent heart attack or other complications. Treatment planning is usually based on an individual’s 10-year or lifetime risk and is developed together with a healthcare provider.

Coronary Heart Disease Lifestyle Changes

Heart-healthy lifestyle changes are recommended as long-term management. A healthy diet includes fruits, vegetables, and whole grains, while limiting saturated fats, sodium, added sugars, and alcohol. Specific dietary approaches such as the DASH (Dietary Approaches to Stop Hypertension) diet and the Therapeutic Lifestyle Changes (TLC) program may be recommended.

Regular physical activity helps manage high cholesterol, high blood pressure, and body weight. Patients are usually advised to consult a healthcare provider before starting a new exercise program and to monitor their activity levels. Quitting smoking is also essential, as smoking damages blood vessels and increases cardiovascular risk. Nicotine and substances in vaping products may also harm the heart and lungs.

Adequate sleep is important for heart health, as it supports the repair of blood vessels. Adults are generally recommended to get 7 to 9 hours of sleep per night. Maintaining a healthy weight helps control conditions such as high cholesterol, diabetes, and high blood pressure. Monitoring blood pressure and cholesterol levels, controlling blood glucose, and managing stress are also important components of long-term care.

Coronary Heart Disease Medications Overview

Medications are used to reduce symptoms such as chest pain and to manage conditions that can worsen coronary heart disease. The treatment plan typically considers all medications being taken, including supplements, and is developed together with a healthcare provider.

Coronary Heart Disease Medications for Blood Pressure and Heart Function

Several types of medications are used to relax blood vessels and reduce the workload on the heart. ACE inhibitors, beta blockers, and angiotensin-receptor blockers help lower blood pressure and decrease strain on the heart. Calcium channel blockers also lower blood pressure by relaxing blood vessels.

Nitrates, such as nitroglycerin, help widen coronary arteries and relieve or prevent angina, which is chest pain caused by reduced blood flow to the heart. Ranolazine may be used to treat coronary microvascular disease and associated chest pain.
