Heart Attack Warning Signs

Heart attack warning signs require immediate medical attention and may appear suddenly or develop gradually. Common symptoms include chest pain or discomfort, often described as pressure, squeezing, fullness, or pain in the center of the chest that lasts for several minutes or comes and goes.

Pain or discomfort may also spread to other areas of the upper body, such as the arms, back, neck, jaw, or stomach. Shortness of breath can occur with or without chest discomfort. Other symptoms include nausea, vomiting, cold sweats, lightheadedness, unusual fatigue, weakness, or anxiety.

Symptoms may vary between individuals, and some people, especially women, may experience less typical signs such as fatigue, shortness of breath, or discomfort in the back or jaw.

If these warning signs occur, immediate medical attention is critical, as early recognition and treatment can reduce serious complications.
